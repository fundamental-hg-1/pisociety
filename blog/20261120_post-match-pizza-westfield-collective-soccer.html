Post-Match Pizza at Westfield Collective During This Weekend's Soccer Showcase

The November 20–22 women’s college soccer showcase brings teams, coaches, and families to Westfield, just one mile north of Westfield Collective.

After hours on the pitch or between showcase matches, stop by PI Society Pizza inside Westfield Collective (750 E. 181st St.) for whole pies, slices, and shareable plates built for post-match refueling.

Weekend Hours at Westfield Collective

  • Friday: 11:00 AM – 10:00 PM
  • Saturday: 10:00 AM – 10:00 PM
  • Sunday: 10:00 AM – 9:00 PM

PI Society Pizza is an independent restaurant concept at Westfield Collective and is not affiliated with or sponsored by Droplight Grand Park Sports Campus or the showcase organizers.
